What this is about

How the experience feels
is part of what you're selling.

Most founders put enormous care into the quality of their work — and much less into the container around it. The intake form, the kickoff call, the check-ins, the way the project ends. These aren't administrative details. They're the client's experience of working with you. And they shape whether clients refer you, return, and trust you with the hard things.

  • Mapping your current client journey end-to-end — from first touchpoint to final delivery
  • Identifying where trust leaks: unclear expectations, awkward transitions, abrupt endings
  • Redesigning the key moments: intake, onboarding, check-ins, offboarding
  • Building lightweight templates and rituals that make the experience consistent without feeling scripted
  • Making it feel like you — not like a generic agency process
