What this is

An online presence that reflects
how good your business already is

Most local businesses know they need a website. Few have the time or interest to build and maintain one themselves — and they shouldn't have to. I take care of the whole thing: figuring out what you need, building it, and setting it up so it just works. You stay focused on your customers; I make sure they can find you.

This might be for you if…

  • You're running a local business — a shop, studio, salon, restaurant, service, or practice — and your online presence doesn't match the quality of what you actually offer
  • You have no website, or one you're embarrassed to share
  • You've looked at website builders and felt overwhelmed or uninterested
  • You want something professional that you don't need to maintain or update constantly
  • You want to show up in local search without having to become a marketing expert

What I take care of

  • A clean, simple website — designed to look good, load fast, and work on any device
  • Your domain and hosting, set up and handled so you don't have to think about it
  • Google Business profile setup so people can find you in local search and on maps
  • Basic SEO — the right words, structure, and metadata so search engines understand what you do and where you are
  • A system that's easy to hand off — so you can make small updates yourself if you ever want to

How it works

Low-lift for you,
start to finish

I handle the complexity. You review, approve, and move on.

1

A short conversation

We talk about your business, your customers, and what you'd want someone to know the moment they land on your site. I ask the right questions so you don't have to figure out what to include.

2

I write it and build it

I draft the copy, design the site, and build it out — all in one pass. You'll see a version to review before anything goes live. Revisions are part of the process.

3

Setup & go live

Domain, hosting, Google Business, search basics — all configured and connected. When we're done, your business is findable, and everything is set up right.

4

You're handed the keys

A short walkthrough of what exists and how to make simple updates if you ever need to — like adding a new service or updating your hours. Nothing complicated required.

This is a good fit for businesses that are ready to show up online properly — and want it done without it becoming a project on their plate.
