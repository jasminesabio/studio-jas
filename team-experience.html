Team & operations

A focused reset for small teams.

For teams that feel scattered, overloaded, or overdue for a reset. We map the current experience, redesign one key workflow or gathering, and build something the team can actually keep using.

What this is

One sprint. One real change
the team can actually keep.

Most small teams have a moment or a workflow that's been quietly dragging them down: a recurring meeting that doesn't work, a handoff that keeps breaking, a gathering that costs more energy than it returns. This sprint surfaces that, redesigns it, and builds a lightweight system around it.

  • A discovery conversation to understand the team, the moment, and what's getting in the way
  • Mapping the current experience to find where things break down or feel off
  • Redesigning one key workflow, gathering, or ritual with the team's actual constraints in mind
  • Building lightweight templates and structures the team can use and adapt on their own
  • A short handoff document so the work doesn't disappear when the sprint ends
