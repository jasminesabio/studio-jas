Who this is for

For teams who are great
at their work — not at software

Software teams have had years to develop their tooling habits. Most non-tech businesses haven't — and the tools keep getting more complex. If you're a creative firm, a wedding planning studio, an event business, or a small service team, you probably didn't sign up to become a project management expert. I help bridge that gap: figuring out which tools are actually worth using, setting them up in a way that makes sense for how you work, and making it all stick.

This might be for you if…

  • You're juggling client work across emails, spreadsheets, sticky notes, and DMs — and something always slips
  • You've tried Asana, Notion, or something similar and it never quite clicked
  • You have a team of 2–10 people and coordination feels harder than it should
  • You're curious about AI tools but don't know where to actually start
  • You want less friction in the back-of-house so you can focus on the actual work

What I help with

  • Picking the right tools for how your team actually works — not the most popular or the most powerful
  • Setting up project and task management so nothing falls through the cracks between clients or events
  • Untangling communication — fewer channels, clearer norms, less ambient noise
  • Introducing AI tools in practical, low-overhead ways that actually save time
  • Building lightweight systems your whole team can use — no tech background required

How it works

Simple, focused,
and built around you

I don't drop in with a prescribed system. We start with how you're actually working and build from there.

1

Starting conversation

We talk through what your current setup looks like — what's working, what's annoying, what keeps falling apart. No jargon, just honest conversation about how your team operates day to day.

2

Audit & recommendations

I look at your tools, workflows, and communication patterns and give you a clear-eyed take on what's worth keeping, what to change, and what to try. You leave with an actual plan — not a list of options.

3

Setup & build

I do the actual configuration work — setting up the structure, templates, and automations — so your team doesn't have to figure it out mid-project. Built for how you work, not for how a template assumes you do.

4

Handoff & support

A short walkthrough for your team so everyone's comfortable, plus a bit of follow-up support while it all settles in. The goal is a system you can actually maintain without me.

This is a focused, standalone engagement — it doesn't need to be tied to anything else. If you've been meaning to get your tools sorted, this is a good place to start.
