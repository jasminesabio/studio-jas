And one more thing

Making things
by hand.

Alongside the facilitation and systems work, I make things. Jewelry right now. Wearable LED work coming. Both are about the same thing: what it feels like to carry something.

The work

Two threads,
one material sensibility

I care about objects that are meant to be used, worn, and lived with — not displayed. Both areas of work start from that same place.

Available now

Jewelry

Considered pieces that are meant to be worn, not displayed. I'm interested in forms that feel right in the hand before they're even on the body — weight, proportion, finish. Not trying too hard to be anything other than what they are.

Reach out if you'd like to see what's available or discuss a custom piece.

In progress

Wearable LED work

I'm slowly building toward wearable pieces that use light as material — not decoration. Light changes how a body reads in space, how presence feels. I'm working through what that means in practice, one prototype at a time.

More here when there's more to show. If this sounds interesting to you, I'd love to talk.
