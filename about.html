About

Hi, I'm Jasmine.

I build small tools with AI for my own life & the people around me: a recipe box, a DJ library, a travel tracker for friends, our wedding.

Studio Jas is where those projects live. I write about how each one came together on Jaslist, my Substack: the why, the how & the hiccups.

Everything here is open to copy. Each Jaslist post ends with a Do it yourself section with the high-level steps, & if a project has a repo, it's linked.

Want something built for you? I also run Curator Labs with Patricia: websites & digital curation for artists & creative businesses.
