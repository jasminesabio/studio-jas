How I work

Listen first. Design from there.

Every engagement starts from the actual state of your team — not a template. I don't arrive with a framework to fit you into.

1

Discovery

We talk about what's actually going on — the team, the moment, the gap. I ask a lot of questions. This conversation shapes everything that follows.

2

Shape

I draft a proposed structure with rationale — arc, sessions, pacing, or system design. We refine it together until it feels right for your specific situation.

3

Build

I handle the details — materials, logistics, setup, configuration — so you can show up present. The preparation work happens behind the scenes.

4

Deliver

I'm there for the actual thing, reading the room and adjusting in real time. No script is more important than what the moment actually needs.

5

Follow through

A synthesis, a handoff, a lightweight ritual to carry the work forward. Whatever shape it takes — the engagement should have a tail.

About

I'm Jasmine — and I care
about how things land.

Studio Jas is a one-person practice. I work at the intersection of experience design, facilitation, and operations — a slightly unusual combination, and that's the point.

My background is in product and ops, but I've always been equally interested in what's happening in the room: who's disengaged, what's unsaid, where the energy goes. That's the thing I bring that doesn't come from a playbook.

I work best with small teams where trust travels fast and the work can be specific to what's actually needed — not a generic solution retrofitted to your situation.
