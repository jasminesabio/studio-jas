How I work

A consistent process,
shaped to fit the moment.

1

Discover

We talk about what's actually going on: the team, the moment, the tension. I ask a lot of questions. This conversation shapes everything that follows.

2

Design

I draft a proposed structure (agenda, workflow, system, or arc) with rationale. We refine it together until it fits your specific situation, not a template.

3

Facilitate

I'm present for the actual experience: reading the room, adjusting in real time, holding the container so participants can focus on the work.

4

Hand off

A synthesis, a lightweight system, or a ritual to carry the work forward. Every engagement ends with something that keeps working after I leave.

About

I'm Jasmine, and I care
about how things land.

Across product work, team collaboration, events, and creative projects, I keep returning to the same question: how does this feel for the people inside it?

My practice is rooted in experience design: shaping workflows, gatherings, and touchpoints so they feel more human, intentional, and alive. I work best with small teams where trust travels fast and the work can be specific to what's actually needed.
