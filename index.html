How I work

A specific process for a specific situation.

Every engagement starts with a real conversation about what's actually going on: the team, the moment, the tension. I ask a lot of questions. From there I draft something shaped to that specific situation — not a template — then I'm present for the actual experience, reading the room and adjusting in real time.

Every engagement ends with something that keeps working after I leave. A synthesis, a lightweight system, or a ritual that carries the work forward.

About

I care about how things land.

Across product work, team collaboration, events, and creative projects, I keep returning to the same question: how does this feel for the people inside it?

My practice is rooted in experience design: shaping workflows, gatherings, and touchpoints so they feel good to be inside of. I work best with small teams where trust travels fast and the work can be specific to what's actually needed.
