Offsite design & facilitation

Two-day reset for a distributed product team

18-person team · Post-reorg · 2 days on-site

Challenge

A product team had just been reorganized and was struggling to find its footing. Roles were unclear, the roadmap felt fragile, and morale had quietly dipped. The offsite needed to do real work — not just boost energy for a week.

Approach

I designed a two-day arc that moved from honest conversation about what wasn't working, through roadmap alignment, to concrete commitments about how the team wanted to operate going forward. Pacing mattered as much as content — enough breathing room for the real conversations to surface.

What I designed

  • Full two-day agenda with facilitated and open-space sessions
  • Workshop structure for surfacing cross-team communication breakdowns
  • Role clarity exercise across four cross-functional squads
  • Closing ritual that converted the day's commitments into standing team practices

Outcome

  • Team left with a clearer roadmap and three new standing rituals
  • Two persistent communication patterns — previously invisible — named and addressed
  • Leader described it as "the first offsite that actually changed something"
Workflow & tool reset

Operations overhaul for a boutique event studio

6-person team · Event planning · 6-week engagement

Challenge

A boutique event studio was running on a patchwork of spreadsheets, email threads, and group chats. Things kept falling through the cracks — not because the team wasn't capable, but because the tools weren't working for how they actually operated.

Approach

I audited their existing workflow, identified the three highest-friction points, and rebuilt the systems that touched those areas. The goal wasn't the most powerful tool — it was the right one, configured for how this team actually works.

What I designed

  • Unified client intake flow replacing four fragmented touchpoints
  • Project tracking system with clear ownership and milestone visibility
  • Internal communication norms to reduce channel noise
  • Team walkthrough and handoff documentation

Outcome

  • Coordination time reduced by roughly half in the first month
  • Every team member has one place to track active events
  • Client onboarding back-and-forth cut by two-thirds
Client experience design

Onboarding redesign for an independent consultant

Solo practitioner · Strategy consulting · 3-week engagement

Challenge

An independent consultant was doing excellent work — but the client experience surrounding it felt like an afterthought. New clients arrived unclear on scope. Project endings felt abrupt. The work was good. The container around it wasn't.

Approach

I mapped the full client journey from first inquiry to final delivery, identifying where trust was leaking at each stage. Then rebuilt the key touchpoints to reflect the quality of the work itself.

What I designed

  • Intake and discovery process that sets expectations before the first call
  • Kickoff structure that establishes scope, rhythm, and working norms
  • Offboarding ritual that creates closure and opens the door for referrals
  • Lightweight templates the consultant can use and adapt independently

Outcome

  • Clients arrive at kickoff with clearer expectations and higher confidence
  • Project endings became a deliberate moment of closure — not a fade-out
  • The consultant stopped dreading intake; the process now does the trust-building for her
