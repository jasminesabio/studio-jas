Offsite design

Two-day reset for a distributed product team

18-person team · Post-reorg · 2 days on-site

A product team had just been reorganized and was struggling to find its footing. Roles were unclear, the roadmap felt fragile, and morale had quietly dipped. The offsite needed to do real work — not just boost energy for a week.

I designed a two-day structure that moved from honest conversation about what wasn't working, through roadmap alignment, to concrete commitments about how the team wanted to operate going forward. The pacing mattered as much as the content: enough breathing room to let the real conversations happen.

  • Left with a clearer roadmap and three standing team rituals
  • Surfaced two persistent communication patterns that had been invisible but costly
  • Facilitated a session that produced concrete role clarity across four cross-functional squads
Workflow design

Operations overhaul for a boutique event studio

6-person team · Event planning · 6-week engagement

A boutique event studio was running on a patchwork of spreadsheets, email threads, and group chats. Things were falling through the cracks — not because the team wasn't capable, but because the tools weren't working for how they actually operated.

I audited their workflow, identified the three biggest friction points, and rebuilt their client intake, project tracking, and internal communication systems from the ground up. The goal wasn't the most powerful tool — it was the right one, set up in a way the whole team would actually use.

  • Reduced coordination time by roughly half in the first month
  • Gave every team member a single place to track active events
  • Built a client onboarding flow that cut back-and-forth emails by two-thirds
Client experience

Onboarding redesign for an independent consultant

Solo practitioner · Strategy consulting · 3-week engagement

An independent consultant was doing excellent work — but the client experience before and after the engagement felt like an afterthought. New clients were arriving unclear on scope, and project endings felt abrupt. The work was good. The container around it wasn't.

I mapped the full client journey from first inquiry to final delivery and identified where the experience was leaking trust. Then rebuilt the key touchpoints: the intake process, the kickoff structure, and the offboarding ritual.

  • Clients arrived at kickoff with clearer expectations and higher confidence
  • Project endings became a deliberate moment of closure and referral opportunity
  • The consultant stopped dreading the intake process — it now does the trust-building for her
