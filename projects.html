Music & workflow

Afterglow

View project →

DJ sets live or die by preparation — knowing what you have, how it feels, and what comes next. But most artists organize their music in folders, which only tells you one thing: genre. Nothing about energy, arc, or where a track belongs in a night.

Afterglow is a track indexing tool that adds the dimensions folders can't: energy level, time of day, part of set (warm up, build, decompress, closing), and mood tags. The goal was to make it faster to find the right track under pressure, and easier to build sets with real intention.

  • Information architecture
  • Tagging systems
  • Workflow design
  • Creative process tooling
Event & logistics

Wedding Website

View project →

My husband and I built two separate sites for our wedding — one for guests, one for our planners — because the information each audience needed was completely different.

The guest site surfaced everything people needed for the full weekend: schedule, venue details, hotel blocks, local recommendations, and real-time updates as plans shifted. The planner site was an ops layer: guest info, transportation logistics, seating assignments, and a minute-by-minute run of show that everyone could actually find and trust.

  • Multi-audience design
  • Event operations
  • Information hierarchy
  • Logistics UX
  • Real-time coordination
Health & systems

Pregnancy Tracker

View project →

The fertility and pregnancy journey generates an enormous amount of information across a long timeline — doctor names, appointment notes, research on diet and lifestyle, workout adjustments, test results. And it's happening to two people at once, not just one.

This tracker centralizes everything in one place, covering the full arc from trying to conceive through postpartum. It also has a layer specifically for my husband: a window into what I'm physically and emotionally experiencing week by week, so he knows how to show up without me having to explain it every time.

  • Journey mapping
  • Emotional experience design
  • Multi-user systems
  • Health information design
Travel & community

Constellaciones

View project →

A group of friends was traveling across Europe the same summer — different cities, different dates — and there was no good way to see who was where and when. Constellaciones is a simple tool that maps everyone's travel schedules so you can instantly see where you overlap.

It also has a marketplace layer for sharing event tickets: if someone can't make it to Movement or Garbicz, they can list their ticket and other friends in the network can claim it. The whole thing is built around the idea that the best moments happen when you realize you're already in the same city.

  • Coordination tools
  • Community experience design
  • Marketplace UX
  • Event logistics
